# 01 — Product North Star

## North-star experience

A user opens Flow and immediately sees the state of their day, unresolved captures, active plans, and open promises.

They enable **Flow Live** once.

They can then say:

> “Inbox.”

> “Renew passport before Senegal.”

> “Turn that into a plan.”

> “Schedule the documents step Friday at ten.”

> “I promised Maya I’d send the proposal by Friday.”

> “What fits right now?”

The environment changes continuously without asking the user to navigate forms, select object types, or repeat the microphone interaction.

## Product principles

### 1. Immediate

The UI acknowledges intent before network or interpretation latency becomes visible.

- target focus begins from interim intent;
- final mutation only occurs after validated final intent;
- common navigation and direct commands execute locally;
- no loading page for ordinary actions.

### 2. Calm

Only one primary transformation owns attention at a time.

- no competing animated widgets;
- no dashboard noise;
- no ambient motion that never settles;
- no celebratory effects for routine productivity actions.

### 3. Contextual

Flow resolves references from the current environment:

- current route;
- focused entity;
- unique title or time;
- most recent capture;
- current event;
- next event;
- open plan;
- named person.

Selection is useful but not required when context already resolves the target.

### 4. Reversible

Every safe operation can be explored without fear.

- atomic transaction;
- one undo step per user intention;
- exact redo;
- what-if mode for consequential changes;
- no silent destructive side effects.

### 5. Honest

Flow does not pretend to understand more than it does.

- no fake progress percentages;
- no fake wellbeing score;
- no hidden inference presented as fact;
- no “AI magic” copy;
- unsupported interpretation returns a concise, actionable reason.

### 6. Unified

Spaces are projections, not databases.

The same plan step may be visible in Plans and Calendar, but it has one identity and one history.

## Everyday retention loops

### Morning

Open Home or Calendar. Tide shows whether the day fits.

### During the day

Use Now to see what fits the current gap or let Tide recover from delay.

### Capture moments

Speak or type an unresolved thought into Inbox from any view.

### Planning moments

Transform selected captures into outcomes and reserve real time.

### Relationship moments

Record a promise once; see it in People, Plans, and Calendar where relevant.

## Success measures for the portfolio release

- First useful state visible in under one second on a normal laptop.
- Common navigation response feels immediate.
- No repeated microphone click during an active session.
- Cross-space transformation begins within one animation frame after commit.
- No unexplained entity duplication or disappearance.
- All major flows are usable with keyboard and reduced motion.
- The user understands where an item went without reading a toast.
