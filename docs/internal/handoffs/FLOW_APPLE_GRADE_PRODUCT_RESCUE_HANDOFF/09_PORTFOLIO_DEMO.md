# 09 — Portfolio Demo

The final portfolio recording should demonstrate one world, not a tour of screens.

## 35-second script

Start on Home with Live Session active.

### 0–4 seconds

Say:

> “I am forty minutes behind. Keep the interview and dinner.”

Home shows a safe recovery preview. Today expands. Fixed events stay anchored; flexible work reflows.

### 4–8 seconds

Say:

> “Book dinner at Pizzeria Roma tomorrow at eight.”

A tomorrow event is created without changing routes unnecessarily.

### 8–11 seconds

Say:

> “Make it red and important.”

The same event updates through conversational context.

### 11–16 seconds

Say:

> “I need to renew my passport before Senegal.”

An Outcome is created. Flow surfaces its next step.

### 16–21 seconds

Say:

> “Schedule the first step Thursday after work.”

The step travels into Today through one clean Tide transition.

### 21–26 seconds

Say:

> “I promised Maya the proposal by Friday.”

A Commitment appears and preparation time is suggested.

### 26–30 seconds

Say:

> “What needs me now?”

One realistic action becomes primary.

### 30–35 seconds

Say:

> “Undo. Redo. Home.”

The state reverses, restores, and returns to the living Home surface without another microphone click.

## Final title

# Flow
## Tell it what changed. Everything relevant falls into place.
