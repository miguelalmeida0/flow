# 00 — Executive Mandate

Flow currently looks like five routes sharing a dark theme and microphone. It does not yet behave like one intelligent world.

This mission is not to add more screens. It is to make the existing product coherent and useful.

## Product promise

> Tell Flow what changed. Everything relevant falls into place.

## Product structure

Flow becomes four clear destinations plus Home:

- **Home** — what matters now and what may break next;
- **Today** — the live day, including Now, calendar, recovery, and breathing room;
- **Capture** — a universal intake that routes a thought to the right destination;
- **Outcomes** — desired results connected to a next action and reserved time;
- **Commitments** — what the user owes, what others owe, and what to remember next time.

Legacy route aliases remain valid during migration:

- `/calendar` → `/today`
- `/inbox` → `/capture`
- `/plans` → `/outcomes`
- `/people` → `/commitments`
- `/now` → `/today?focus=now`

## Immediate product standard

The app must feel immediate, predictable, and calm:

- one microphone activation per foreground session;
- global voice understanding from every route;
- no command fails because the user is on the wrong page;
- no unsupported phrase creates data;
- no permanent error panels;
- no empty page whose only job is explaining itself;
- one underlying state model and one action engine;
- every safe change is reversible;
- every page has a critical reason to exist;
- motion explains change instead of decorating it.

## Priority order

1. Correctness and trust
2. Global voice continuity
3. Complete utility loops
4. Cross-space coherence
5. UX clarity
6. Motion quality
7. Visual polish
8. Mascot, only later

The mascot handoff is explicitly paused until this mission is release-green.
