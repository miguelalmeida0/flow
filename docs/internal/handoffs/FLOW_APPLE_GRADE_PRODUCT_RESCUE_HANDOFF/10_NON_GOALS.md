# 10 — Non-goals

Do not add during this mission:

- GPT or another paid LLM runtime;
- arbitrary natural-language promises the deterministic engine cannot keep;
- image generation;
- Three.js, WebGL, shaders, or canvas-only UI;
- authentication;
- backend services;
- cloud synchronization;
- Google/Apple calendar provider integration;
- contacts integration;
- email sending;
- purchasing/reservations;
- recurrence engine beyond existing proven scope;
- time-zone collaboration;
- full CRM;
- Kanban boards;
- streaks, XP, leaderboards, productivity scores, or guilt mechanics;
- mascot implementation;
- multilingual parser expansion;
- system-wide background microphone behavior in a browser tab.

The product must first become coherent, useful, reliable, and beautiful in its current local portfolio scope.
